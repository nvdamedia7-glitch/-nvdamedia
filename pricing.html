FAQ

Common questions

What's your average turnaround time?

Most projects are delivered in 3-7 business days depending on your plan. Premium clients get 24-48 hour rush delivery.

Do you shoot footage or only edit?

We primarily edit footage you provide, but we also offer drone and on-site filming add-ons in select locations — ask us when you enquire.

Can I cancel or change my plan anytime?

Yes. Plans are billed monthly with no long-term lock-in, and you can upgrade, downgrade or pause between billing cycles.

What file formats do you deliver?

We deliver MP4 (H.264/H.265) by default, with ProRes or platform-specific export sizes available on request.

Do unused videos roll over to next month?

Up to 2 unused videos can roll over one billing cycle on Professional and Premium plans.
